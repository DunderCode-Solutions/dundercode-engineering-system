# 1. Purpose

The Engineering Templates Overview defines the vision, purpose, and organization of the DunderCode Engineering Templates (DET).

Its purpose is to establish a standardized library of reusable engineering templates that support the complete software engineering lifecycle while maintaining consistency, traceability, quality, and governance.

DET transforms engineering knowledge into standardized engineering artifacts.

---

# 2. Scope

DET covers reusable templates for engineering documentation, including:

* Project documentation
* Requirements documentation
* Architecture documentation
* API specifications
* Testing documentation
* Operational documentation
* AI documentation
* Governance documentation

DET standardizes engineering artifacts but does not replace engineering judgment.

---

# 3. Audience

This template library is intended for:

# 5. Engineering Principles

Every engineering template SHALL follow the principles below.

## Standardization

Templates SHALL promote consistent engineering documentation.

---

## Reusability

Templates SHALL maximize reuse across engineering projects.

---

## Simplicity

Templates SHOULD remain concise and easy to adopt.

---

## Completeness

Templates SHOULD include sufficient guidance to support engineering work without becoming unnecessarily prescriptive.

---

## Traceability

Templates SHALL preserve traceability to engineering standards, architectures, requirements, and decisions.

---

## Technology Independence

Templates SHOULD remain independent of implementation technologies whenever practical.

---

## Maintainability

Templates SHOULD be easy to update as engineering practices evolve.

---

## Evolvability

Templates SHALL evolve through controlled engineering governance.

---

## Readability

Templates SHOULD prioritize clarity for both human engineers and AI-assisted systems.

---

## Practicality

Templates SHALL facilitate engineering execution rather than increase unnecessary documentation effort.

An engineering template complies with this standard when it:

* Follows DET engineering principles.
* Supports standardized engineering documentation.
* Preserves engineering traceability.
* Remains reusable across projects.
* Supports continuous engineering improvement.
