# DunderCode Engineering Templates (DET)

---

# 1. Purpose

The DunderCode Engineering Templates (DET) is the official engineering template library of the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Its purpose is to provide standardized, reusable templates for engineering artifacts used throughout the software lifecycle.

DET transforms engineering standards and architectural guidance into practical documentation assets that promote consistency, quality, traceability, and engineering efficiency.

---

# 2. Scope

DET covers reusable engineering templates, including:

* Project Templates
* Requirements Templates
* Architecture Templates
* API Templates
* Testing Templates
* Operational Templates
* AI Templates
* Governance Templates

DET does **not** define engineering standards.

Engineering standards are defined by **DES**.

DET does **not** define engineering architectures.

Engineering architectures are defined by **DEA**.

DET does **not** define engineering assessments.

Engineering assessments are defined by **DAR**.

---

# 3. Audience

DET is intended for:

* Software Engineers
* Software Architects
* Solution Architects
* Technical Leaders
* Product Managers
* Engineering Managers
* QA Engineers
* DevOps Engineers
* Documentation Engineers
* AI-assisted engineering systems

# 4. Relationship with DESys

DET is the engineering artifact layer of DESys.

```text id="ry78nq"
Engineering Philosophy (DEC)
            ↓

            ↓
Engineering Standards (DES)
            ↓
Engineering Assessment (DAR)
            ↓
Engineering Architecture (DEA)
            ↓
Engineering Templates (DET)
            ↓
Engineering Projects
```

DET operationalizes engineering knowledge through standardized documentation.

---

# 5. Engineering Philosophy

DET follows these principles:

* Standardization before customization.
* Reuse before recreation.
* Simplicity before complexity.
* Consistency across engineering artifacts.
* Traceability throughout the engineering lifecycle.
* Technology independence whenever practical.
* Documentation as an engineering asset.
* Continuous evolution through governance.

---

# 13. Governance

All DET assets SHALL:

* be version controlled;
* remain traceable to DES standards;
* align with DEA architectures whenever applicable;
* be technically reviewed before publication;
* evolve through controlled engineering governance.

Templates SHOULD continuously improve based on engineering experience and organizational feedback.

---

# 14. Versioning

DET follows the same governance model adopted across DESys:

* Canonical identifiers
* Semantic versioning
* Controlled evolution
* Technical review
* Engineering traceability
