# 1. Purpose

The Template Governance Standard defines the engineering governance model for creating, maintaining, reviewing, versioning, and evolving reusable engineering templates within the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Its purpose is to ensure that engineering templates remain consistent, reusable, traceable, technically accurate, and aligned with engineering standards and architecture throughout their lifecycle.

Template governance preserves the integrity of the engineering documentation ecosystem.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to every engineering template published within DET.

It defines governance expectations for:

* Template ownership
* Template lifecycle
* Version management
* Technical review
* Change management
* Traceability
* Publication
* Deprecation
* Continuous improvement

Project-specific documentation governance is intentionally outside the scope of this document.

# 5. Engineering Governance Principles

Every template governance activity SHALL follow the principles below.

## Ownership

Every engineering template SHALL have an identified owner responsible for its lifecycle.

---

## Traceability

Templates SHALL remain traceable to DES standards, DEA architectures, and engineering practices.

---

## Standardization

Templates SHALL follow consistent structures and documentation conventions.

---

## Technical Accuracy

Templates SHALL be technically reviewed before publication.

---

## Version Control

Templates SHALL support semantic versioning and documented evolution.

---

## Reusability

Governance SHOULD maximize template reuse across engineering initiatives.

---

## Transparency

Template changes SHALL be documented and auditable.

---

## Controlled Evolution

Templates SHALL evolve through governed engineering processes.

---

## Quality Assurance

Templates SHOULD undergo periodic quality reviews to ensure continued relevance.

---

## Continuous Improvement

Template governance SHALL encourage improvement based on engineering experience and organizational feedback.

---

# 6. Governance Responsibilities

Template governance SHOULD define responsibilities for:

* Template ownership
* Template creation
* Technical review
* Version approval
* Publication
* Repository maintenance
* Change management
* Template retirement

Governance responsibilities MAY be distributed across engineering governance bodies.

---

# 7. Mandatory Requirements

Every governed template MUST:

* Have a unique canonical identifier.
* Have a documented owner.
* Follow DET documentation standards.
* Preserve engineering traceability.
* Support semantic versioning.
* Include a changelog.
* Be technically reviewed before publication.
* Support future evolution.

---

# 8. Template Governance Lifecycle

Engineering templates SHALL follow a controlled lifecycle.

```text id="p7v3kc"
Engineering Need
        ↓
Template Design
        ↓
Technical Review
        ↓
Approval
        ↓
Publication
        ↓
Engineering Adoption
        ↓
Operational Feedback
        ↓
Template Evolution
```

Templates SHALL continuously improve as engineering practices mature.

---

# 9. Compliance

A template complies with this standard when it:

* Has documented ownership.
* Preserves engineering traceability.
* Follows DET standards.
* Supports version control.
* Has undergone technical review.
* Remains reusable and maintainable.
