# 1. Purpose

The Engineering Processes Overview establishes the conceptual foundation of the DunderCode Engineering Processes (DEP) library.

Its purpose is to define the role of engineering processes within the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys), describe how engineering work is executed, and provide a standardized framework for the software engineering lifecycle.

DEP transforms engineering standards into disciplined execution.

---

# 2. Scope

# 5. Engineering Process Philosophy

DEP follows the following engineering principles.

## Engineering First

Processes exist to support engineering, not bureaucracy.

---

## Repeatability

Engineering activities should produce predictable outcomes.

---

## Traceability

Every engineering activity should be traceable throughout the software lifecycle.

---

## Simplicity

Processes should remain as simple as possible while preserving engineering quality.

---

## Continuous Improvement

Engineering processes should continuously evolve through experience.

---

## Automation

Manual activities should be automated whenever practical.

---

## Collaboration

Engineering is a collaborative discipline involving multiple roles and stakeholders.

---

## Governance

Engineering processes shall support organizational governance without reducing engineering agility.

# 9. Compliance

An engineering process complies with DEP when it:

* Aligns with DES engineering standards.
* Supports repeatable execution.
* Preserves engineering traceability.
* Clearly defines responsibilities.
* Supports engineering governance.
* Enables continuous improvement.
