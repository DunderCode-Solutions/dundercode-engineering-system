# 1. Purpose

The Architecture Governance Support Standard defines the engineering governance model for managing architectural assets throughout their lifecycle within the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Its purpose is to ensure that engineering architectures remain consistent, traceable, reusable, evolvable, and aligned with organizational engineering standards.

Architecture governance provides the control framework that preserves architectural integrity across all engineering initiatives.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to every engineering architecture published within DEA.

It defines engineering expectations for:

* Architecture ownership
* Governance responsibilities
* Architectural reviews
* Decision governance
* Version management
* Traceability
* Compliance
* Continuous architectural evolution

Project management processes are intentionally outside the scope of this document.

# 5. Engineering Governance Principles

Every architecture governance process SHALL follow the principles below.

## Accountability

Every architectural asset SHALL have clearly identified ownership.

Architectural responsibilities MUST remain explicit.

---

## Traceability

Architectural decisions, reviews, and revisions SHALL remain fully traceable.

---

## Consistency

Architecture governance SHALL promote consistent engineering practices across projects.

---

## Compliance

Architectural assets SHALL remain aligned with DES engineering standards.

---

## Controlled Evolution

Architectures SHALL evolve through governed engineering processes.

Uncontrolled architectural drift SHOULD be avoided.

---

## Transparency

Governance decisions SHOULD be documented and understandable.

Review rationale SHOULD remain available for future reference.

---

## Reusability

Governance SHOULD encourage reuse before creating new architectural assets.

---

## Reviewability

Architectural assets SHALL be periodically reviewed to ensure continued relevance.

---

## Continuous Improvement

Architecture governance SHALL continuously improve through engineering feedback.

---

## Engineering Alignment

Governance SHALL ensure alignment between engineering strategy and architectural implementation.

---

# 6. Governance Responsibilities

Architecture governance SHOULD define responsibilities for:

* Architecture ownership
* Architecture publication
* Technical review
* Version approval
* Change management
* Compliance verification
* Repository maintenance
* Architecture retirement

Responsibilities MAY be distributed across architecture governance bodies.

---

# 7. Mandatory Requirements

Every architecture governed under DEA MUST:

* Have an identified owner.
* Be version controlled.
* Preserve engineering traceability.
* Undergo technical review.
* Support controlled evolution.
* Maintain alignment with DES standards.
* Be periodically reassessed for continued relevance.

---

# 8. Governance Lifecycle

Architecture governance SHALL follow a continuous lifecycle.

```text id="z4n6kc"
Architecture Definition
        ↓

        ↓
Approval
        ↓
Publication
        ↓
Project Adoption
        ↓
Operational Feedback
        ↓
Governance Review
        ↓

```

Governance SHALL ensure architectural quality throughout the complete lifecycle.

---

# 9. Compliance

An architectural asset complies with this standard when it:

* Has defined ownership.
* Preserves architectural traceability.
* Supports governance reviews.
* Aligns with DES engineering standards.
* Evolves through controlled engineering processes.
* Remains reusable and maintainable.
