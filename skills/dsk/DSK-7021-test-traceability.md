# 1. Purpose

This skill defines the **Engineering Test Traceability (ETT)** discipline adopted by the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Within DESys, Test Traceability is not a Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM) or a collection of links between artifacts.

It is the engineering discipline responsible for establishing, preserving and governing the complete Digital Engineering Thread that connects engineering requirements, behavioral models, experiments, executions, evidence, analytics, reports and engineering decisions.

Engineering Test Traceability transforms isolated engineering artifacts into an integrated engineering knowledge network.
