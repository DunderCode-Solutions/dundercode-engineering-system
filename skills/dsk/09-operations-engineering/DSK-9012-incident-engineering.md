# 1. Purpose

This skill defines the **Incident Engineering (IE)** discipline adopted by the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Within DESys, Incident Engineering is not the handling of operational tickets, emergency response or production troubleshooting.

It is the engineering discipline responsible for detecting, coordinating, mitigating, recovering, analyzing and continuously learning from operational incidents throughout their complete engineering lifecycle.

Incident Engineering transforms operational incidents into engineering knowledge.

---

# 2. Scope

Incident Engineering governs:

* Incident Detection
* Incident Classification
* Incident Coordination
* Incident Response
* Incident Recovery
* Incident Analysis

Incident Engineering spans every operational incident affecting engineering capabilities.
