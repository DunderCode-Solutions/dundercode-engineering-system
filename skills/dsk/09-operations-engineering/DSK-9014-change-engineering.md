# DSK-9014 | Change Engineering

# 1. Purpose

This skill defines the **Change Engineering (CE)** discipline adopted by the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Within DESys, Change Engineering is not the approval of change requests, CAB meetings or administrative change control.

It is the engineering discipline responsible for designing, evaluating, validating, executing, observing and continuously improving engineering changes throughout their complete operational lifecycle.

Change Engineering transforms operational change into a governed engineering capability.

---

# 2. Scope

Change Engineering governs:

* Change Modeling
* Change Planning
* Change Risk Engineering
* Change Validation
* Change Execution
* Change Recovery
* Change Learning
* Change Evolution

Change Engineering spans every engineering change affecting operational capabilities.

# 5. Change Engineering Model (CEM)

DESys adopts the **Change Engineering Model (CEM)**.

Every engineering change SHALL define:

* Change Identifier
* Change Objective
* Change Scope
* Affected Services
* Change Category
* Risk Assessment
* Impact Assessment
* Validation Strategy
* Rollback Strategy
* Success Criteria
* Engineering Evidence
* Traceability

The CEM defines the canonical engineering change model adopted by DESys.
