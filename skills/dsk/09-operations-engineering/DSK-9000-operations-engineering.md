# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the canonical Operations Engineering discipline adopted by the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Within DESys, Operations Engineering is not the execution of operational activities, infrastructure administration or production support.

It is the engineering discipline responsible for preserving, operating, observing, improving and continuously evolving engineering capabilities after they become operational services.

Operations Engineering transforms operational systems into continuously sustainable engineering capabilities.

---

# 2. Vision

DESys defines Operations Engineering as the discipline responsible for ensuring that engineering capabilities continuously deliver business value throughout their operational lifecycle.

Operations Engineering preserves operational confidence through engineering.

---

# 3. Mission

The mission of Operations Engineering is to:

* sustain operational engineering capabilities;
* maximize operational reliability;
* strengthen organizational resilience;
* enable evidence-driven operational decisions;
* preserve Engineering Excellence throughout service operation.

Operations Engineering continuously protects the value created by Engineering Delivery.
