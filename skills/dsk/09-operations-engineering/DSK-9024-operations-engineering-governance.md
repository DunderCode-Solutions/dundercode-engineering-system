# DSK-9024 | Operations Engineering Governance

# 1. Purpose

This skill establishes the canonical governance model for the complete **Engineering Operations System (EOS)** adopted by the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Within DESys, Operations Engineering Governance is not an operational management function, an audit process or a compliance framework.

It is the engineering discipline responsible for governing, integrating, measuring, evolving and continuously improving every operational engineering capability throughout the complete Engineering Operations System.

Operations Engineering Governance transforms Operations Engineering into a continuously governed engineering system.

Operations Engineering Governance spans the complete Operations Engineering Domain.
