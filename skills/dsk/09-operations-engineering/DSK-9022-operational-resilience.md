# DSK-9022 | Operational Resilience Engineering

# 1. Purpose

This skill defines the **Operational Resilience Engineering (ORE)** discipline adopted by the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Within DESys, Operational Resilience Engineering is not disaster recovery, business continuity planning or fault tolerance alone.

It is the engineering discipline responsible for designing, validating, preserving and continuously improving the capability of engineering systems to resist, absorb, adapt, recover and evolve under expected and unexpected operational disruptions.

Operational Resilience Engineering transforms operational disruption into engineering adaptation.

---

Operational Resilience Engineering governs:

* Engineering Resilience
* Service Resilience
* Adaptive Engineering
* Graceful Degradation
* Recovery Engineering
* Business Continuity
* Organizational Learning
* Continuous Resilience Evolution

Operational Resilience Engineering spans every operational engineering capability.
