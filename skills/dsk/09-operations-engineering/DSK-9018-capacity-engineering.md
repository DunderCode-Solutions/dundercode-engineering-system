# DSK-9018 | Capacity Engineering

# 1. Purpose

This skill defines the **Capacity Engineering (CE)** discipline adopted by the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Within DESys, Capacity Engineering is not infrastructure sizing, resource provisioning or capacity planning spreadsheets.

It is the engineering discipline responsible for modeling, forecasting, validating, optimizing and continuously evolving the operational capacity required to sustain engineering services under present and future demand.

Capacity Engineering transforms capacity into a continuously governed engineering capability.

---

Capacity Engineering governs:

* Capacity Modeling
* Capacity Forecasting
* Capacity Analysis
* Bottleneck Engineering
* Capacity Optimization
* Capacity Scaling
* Capacity Intelligence
* Continuous Capacity Evolution

Capacity Engineering spans every operational engineering capability.
