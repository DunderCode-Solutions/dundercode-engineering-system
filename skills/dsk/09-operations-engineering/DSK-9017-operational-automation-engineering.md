# 1. Purpose

This skill defines the **Operational Automation Engineering (OAE)** discipline adopted by the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Within DESys, Operational Automation Engineering is not the execution of scripts, scheduled jobs or infrastructure automation.

It is the engineering discipline responsible for designing, orchestrating, executing, validating and continuously evolving autonomous operational actions that preserve service health, operational reliability and Engineering Excellence.

Operational Automation Engineering transforms operational knowledge into autonomous engineering capabilities.

# 17. Engineering Rules

Operational Automation Engineering MUST:

* automate repetitive engineering activities;
* preserve policy-governed execution;
* validate autonomous actions;
* maintain engineering evidence;
* continuously improve automation maturity.

Operational Automation Engineering MUST NOT:

* execute unmanaged automations;
* bypass governance policies;
* compromise operational safety;
* perform irreversible actions without safeguards;
* weaken engineering explainability.
