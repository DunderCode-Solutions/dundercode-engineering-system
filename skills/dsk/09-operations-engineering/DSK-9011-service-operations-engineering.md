# 1. Purpose

This skill defines the **Service Operations Engineering (SOE)** discipline adopted by the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Within DESys, Service Operations Engineering is not the execution of operational tasks, ticket handling or production support.

It is the engineering discipline responsible for designing, operating, observing, maintaining and continuously evolving operational services throughout their complete operational lifecycle.

Service Operations Engineering transforms operational services into continuously sustainable engineering capabilities.

---
