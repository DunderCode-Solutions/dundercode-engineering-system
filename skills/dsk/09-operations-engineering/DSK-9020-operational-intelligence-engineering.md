# 1. Purpose

This skill defines the **Operational Intelligence Engineering (OIE)** discipline adopted by the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Within DESys, Operational Intelligence Engineering is not reporting, dashboards, business intelligence or isolated AIOps analytics.

It is the engineering discipline responsible for transforming operational evidence into engineering knowledge, predictions, recommendations and explainable operational decisions that continuously improve the Engineering Operations System.

Operational Intelligence Engineering transforms operational data into engineering intelligence.
