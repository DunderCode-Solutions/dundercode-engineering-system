# 1. Purpose

This skill defines the **Availability Engineering (AE)** discipline adopted by the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Within DESys, Availability Engineering is not uptime monitoring, infrastructure redundancy or SLA reporting.

It is the engineering discipline responsible for designing, measuring, validating, preserving and continuously improving the operational availability of engineering services throughout their complete lifecycle.

Availability Engineering transforms service availability into a measurable engineering capability.
