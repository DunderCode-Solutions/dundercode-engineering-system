# DSK-9021 | Operational Traceability Engineering

# 1. Purpose

This skill defines the **Operational Traceability Engineering (OTE)** discipline adopted by the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Within DESys, Operational Traceability Engineering is not audit logging, activity history or compliance reporting.

It is the engineering discipline responsible for preserving, correlating, explaining and continuously evolving the complete chain of operational evidence across every engineering capability throughout the operational lifecycle.

Operational Traceability Engineering transforms isolated operational events into explainable engineering history.
