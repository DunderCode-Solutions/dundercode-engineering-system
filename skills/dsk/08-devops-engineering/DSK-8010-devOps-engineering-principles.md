# DSK-8010 | DevOps Engineering Principles

# 1. Purpose

This skill defines the **Engineering Principles** that govern DevOps Engineering within the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Within DESys, DevOps principles are not operational recommendations or tool-specific best practices.

They are the immutable engineering foundations that govern how engineering value continuously flows from strategy to production while preserving automation, reliability, security, observability and organizational learning.

Engineering principles SHALL remain independent of technologies, vendors and implementation platforms.

---

# 2. Scope

DevOps Engineering Principles govern:

* Engineering Culture
* Engineering Flow
* Engineering Automation
* Engineering Reliability
* Engineering Feedback
* Engineering Observability
* Engineering Learning
* Engineering Governance

These principles apply to every DevOps Engineering capability.

# 6. Canonical Engineering Principles

DESys adopts the following canonical principles.

## Principle 1 — Engineering Before Operations

Operations SHALL be engineered.

Manual operational activities SHALL continuously evolve into engineered capabilities.

---

## Principle 2 — Continuous Value Flow

Engineering work SHALL continuously move from idea to production.

Artificial bottlenecks SHALL be eliminated.

---

## Principle 3 — Automation by Default

Every repetitive engineering activity SHOULD become automated.

Automation SHALL reduce variability while improving consistency.

---

## Principle 4 — Reliability Before Speed

Delivery speed SHALL never compromise engineering reliability.

Reliability is a prerequisite for sustainable velocity.

---

## Principle 5 — Shift Quality Everywhere

Quality SHALL exist throughout the Engineering Delivery System.

Quality is never a final validation phase.

---

## Principle 6 — Security by Engineering

Security SHALL be integrated into every engineering capability.

Security SHALL continuously evolve with delivery.

---

## Principle 7 — Observability by Design

Every engineering capability SHALL be observable.

Engineering decisions SHALL rely on measurable evidence.

---

## Principle 8 — Feedback Drives Evolution

Operational feedback SHALL continuously improve engineering systems.

Learning SHALL become part of engineering.

---

## Principle 9 — Shared Engineering Ownership

Engineering responsibility SHALL be collective.

Development, Quality, Security, Platform and Operations share accountability for engineering outcomes.

---

## Principle 10 — Continuous Engineering

Engineering SHALL continuously evolve through experimentation, automation, measurement and organizational learning.

Continuous improvement becomes an engineering capability.
