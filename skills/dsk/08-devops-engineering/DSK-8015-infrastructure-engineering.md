# 1. Purpose

This skill defines the **Infrastructure Engineering (IE)** discipline adopted by the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Within DESys, Infrastructure Engineering is not the provisioning or administration of servers, networks or cloud resources.

It is the engineering discipline responsible for designing, building, operating and continuously evolving the computational capabilities that sustain the complete Engineering Delivery System.

Infrastructure Engineering transforms infrastructure into an engineering capability.

---

Infrastructure Engineering governs:

Infrastructure Engineering spans every computational capability required by the Engineering Delivery System.
