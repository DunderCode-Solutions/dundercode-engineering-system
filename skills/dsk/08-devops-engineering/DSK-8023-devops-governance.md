# DSK-8023 | DevOps Governance

# 1. Purpose

This skill defines the **DevOps Governance (DG)** discipline adopted by the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Within DESys, DevOps Governance is not the administration of DevOps tools, approval workflows or operational policies.

It is the engineering discipline responsible for governing, coordinating and continuously improving the complete Engineering Delivery System through engineering policies, automated controls, organizational accountability and evidence-based decision making.

DevOps Governance transforms Engineering Delivery into a governed organizational capability.

---

# 2. Scope

DevOps Governance governs:

* Engineering Delivery Governance
* Delivery Policies
* Delivery Controls
* Delivery Compliance
* Delivery Decision Management
* Engineering Accountability
* Continuous Delivery Improvement
* Delivery Intelligence

DevOps Governance spans the complete Engineering Delivery lifecycle.
