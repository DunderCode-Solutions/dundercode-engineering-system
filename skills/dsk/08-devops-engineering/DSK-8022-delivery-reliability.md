# DSK-8022 | Delivery Reliability

# 1. Purpose

This skill defines the **Delivery Reliability (DR)** discipline adopted by the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Within DESys, Delivery Reliability is not the measurement of deployment success or pipeline availability.

It is the engineering discipline responsible for ensuring that the complete Engineering Delivery System consistently delivers engineering capabilities safely, predictably, recoverably and sustainably.

Delivery Reliability transforms engineering delivery into a continuously trustworthy organizational capability.
