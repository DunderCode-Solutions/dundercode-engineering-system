# DSK-8017 | Platform Engineering

# 1. Purpose

This skill defines the **Platform Engineering (PE)** discipline adopted by the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Within DESys, Platform Engineering is not the management of Kubernetes clusters, cloud services or developer portals.

It is the engineering discipline responsible for designing, building, operating and continuously evolving the Engineering Platform that provides secure, standardized, self-service engineering capabilities to software teams.

Platform Engineering transforms engineering infrastructure into a consumable engineering product.

---

# 2. Scope

Platform Engineering governs:

* Engineering Platforms
* Internal Developer Platform (IDP)
* Self-Service Engineering
* Golden Paths
* Developer Experience
* Platform Automation
* Platform Governance
* Platform Reliability

Platform Engineering spans the complete Engineering Delivery System.

* secure self-service engineering;
