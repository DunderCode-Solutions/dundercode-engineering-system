# 1. Purpose

This skill defines the **DevOps Engineering Governance (DEG)** discipline adopted by the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Within DESys, DevOps Engineering Governance is not the governance of isolated DevOps activities, delivery pipelines or operational controls.

It is the canonical engineering discipline responsible for governing the complete DevOps Engineering ecosystem, ensuring that engineering delivery, infrastructure, platforms, automation, operational intelligence, organizational learning and continuous improvement remain permanently aligned with Engineering Excellence.

DevOps Engineering Governance establishes the constitutional framework of DevOps Engineering within DESys.
