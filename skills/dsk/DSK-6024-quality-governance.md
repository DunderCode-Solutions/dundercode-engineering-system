# 1. Purpose

This skill defines the **Engineering Quality Governance (EQG)** discipline adopted by the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Within DESys, Quality Governance is not limited to audits, approvals or organizational oversight.

It is the engineering discipline responsible for establishing, coordinating, measuring and continuously evolving the complete Engineering Quality System through policies, standards, governance controls, engineering evidence and organizational accountability.

Engineering Quality Governance sustains Engineering Excellence.
