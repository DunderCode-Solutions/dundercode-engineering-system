# 1. Purpose

This document introduces the Design Skills of the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Design Engineering transforms validated business models and software architecture into maintainable, extensible and implementation-ready software designs.

It bridges the gap between Domain Engineering and Software Engineering by defining how business concepts should be expressed through software structures without introducing business behavior.

---

# 2. Scope

The Design Skills cover:

* Software Design Principles
* Object-Oriented Design
* Design Patterns
* Dependency Management
* Interface Design
* API Design
* Package Organization
* Modular Design
* Coupling and Cohesion
* Refactoring
* Code Quality
* Design Reviews
* Design Traceability

---

# 3. Design Philosophy

DESys Design Engineering is guided by the following principles:

* business behavior belongs to the Domain Model;
* design expresses, but never defines, business rules;
* architecture establishes boundaries;
* design structures implementation;
* implementation follows design;
* maintainability takes precedence over convenience;
* simplicity is preferred over unnecessary abstraction.

---

# 4. Objectives

The Design Skills aim to:

* transform domain models into implementation-ready designs;
* improve maintainability and extensibility;
* promote low coupling and high cohesion;
* encourage consistent software structures;
* standardize software design decisions;
* preserve engineering traceability.

---

# 5. Relationship with Other Engineering Areas

Design Engineering builds upon previous engineering disciplines.

```text
Requirements Engineering
        ↓
Architecture Engineering
        ↓
Domain Engineering
        ↓
Design Engineering
        ↓
Software Engineering
        ↓
Testing Engineering
```

Design Engineering never replaces Domain Engineering.

Instead, it provides the structural software representation of the domain.

# 7. Engineering Principles

Every Design Skill should:

* preserve architectural boundaries;
* remain technology independent whenever possible;
* express domain concepts clearly;
* minimize accidental complexity;
* maximize readability and maintainability;
* support long-term software evolution.

---

# 8. Collaboration

The Design Skills collaborate with:

* Requirements Engineering
* Architecture Engineering
* Domain Engineering
* Software Engineering
* Testing Engineering

They provide the structural foundation required for software implementation while preserving alignment with business and architectural decisions.

---

# 9. Expected Outcomes

After completing the Design Skills, engineering teams should obtain:

* implementation-ready software designs;
* standardized software structures;
* consistent application of design principles;
* reusable architectural patterns;
* improved maintainability;
* reduced technical debt;
* complete engineering traceability.

The Design Skills establish the software design layer of DESys, ensuring that business concepts are translated into clean, modular and maintainable software structures while preserving consistency across the entire engineering lifecycle.
