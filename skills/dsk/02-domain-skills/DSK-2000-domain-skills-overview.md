# DSK-2000 | Domain Skills Overview

# 1. Purpose

The Domain Skills series defines how AI agents understand, analyze and model business knowledge within the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

These skills establish the engineering practices required to transform business understanding into structured domain knowledge before architectural or implementation decisions are made.

Domain Skills represent the business knowledge foundation of the DESys engineering lifecycle.

---

# 2. Scope

The Domain Skills series supports:

* Business Domain Analysis
* Ubiquitous Language
* Domain Discovery
* Business Capability Modeling
* Business Process Modeling
* Domain Event Modeling
* Domain Services
* Value Objects
* Aggregates
* Domain Policies
* Domain Validation
* Domain Traceability

---

# 3. Objectives

The Domain Skills series aims to:

* understand business domains;
* establish shared terminology;
* organize business knowledge;
* identify business capabilities;
* model business behavior;
* support software architecture;
* improve engineering consistency.

---

# 4. Position Within DESys

The Domain Skills series bridges business understanding and software engineering.

Typical engineering flow:

```text id="6ij4sk"
Business Vision

↓

Domain Skills

↓

Requirements Engineering

↓

Architecture Engineering

↓

Solution Design

↓

Implementation

↓

Testing

↓

Operations
```

Domain understanding should always precede architectural and implementation activities.

---

# 5. Engineering Philosophy

DESys adopts Domain-Driven Design (DDD) principles as the primary approach for business modeling.

Key principles include:

* business-first thinking;
* shared ubiquitous language;
* explicit business boundaries;
* business capability orientation;
* traceable domain knowledge;
* continuous collaboration between domain experts and engineering teams.

Technology should serve the business domain rather than define it.

---

# 9. Dependencies

The Domain Skills series collaborates closely with:

* Requirements Engineering
* Architecture Engineering
* Product Engineering
* Software Design
* Quality Assurance

The resulting domain knowledge becomes an input for subsequent engineering activities.

---

# 10. Engineering Principles

Every Domain Skill should:

* prioritize business understanding;
* preserve business terminology;
* avoid technology bias;
* document business knowledge explicitly;
* support continuous evolution;
* preserve engineering traceability.

Business concepts should remain stable even when implementation technologies evolve.

---

# 11. Expected Outcomes

After completing the Domain Skills series, AI agents should be able to:

* understand business terminology;
* identify business capabilities;
* model domain behavior;
* recognize domain boundaries;
* establish ubiquitous language;
* support architecture and software design using consistent business knowledge.

The Domain Skills series establishes the business knowledge foundation of the DESys engineering lifecycle, ensuring that all subsequent engineering decisions remain aligned with real business needs while preserving long-term consistency, traceability and maintainability.
