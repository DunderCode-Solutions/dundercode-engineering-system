# 1. Purpose

This skill defines the **Continuous Quality Engineering (CQE)** discipline adopted by the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Within DESys, quality is not considered a final achievement or release milestone.

It is a continuously evolving engineering capability that transforms evidence, measurements, operational feedback and engineering learning into permanent improvements across the complete engineering lifecycle.

Continuous Quality Engineering sustains engineering excellence.

---

Continuous Quality Engineering governs:

* Continuous Improvement

Continuous Quality Engineering spans the complete engineering lifecycle.

* define measurable improvement objectives;
* institutionalize validated improvements;
* maintain engineering traceability;
