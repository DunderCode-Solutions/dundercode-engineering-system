# DSK-6000 | Quality Engineering Overview

# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the **Quality Engineering** discipline adopted by the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Within DESys, quality is not a final verification activity nor a testing phase.

Quality is an engineering discipline responsible for ensuring that engineering assets continuously satisfy their intended objectives through measurable, verifiable and continuously improving engineering practices.

Engineering Quality protects engineering excellence.

---

# 2. Scope

Quality Engineering governs:

* Engineering Quality Principles
* Verification
* Validation
* Testing Engineering
* Quality Measurement
* Defect Engineering
* Reliability Engineering
* Quality Governance
* Continuous Improvement
* Engineering Traceability

Quality Engineering spans the complete engineering lifecycle.

---

The EQM defines the canonical quality model adopted by DESys.

# 13. Engineering Rules

Quality Engineering MUST:

* define measurable quality objectives;
* preserve engineering evidence;
* support continuous verification;
* preserve engineering traceability;
* continuously improve engineering quality.

Quality Engineering MUST NOT:

* depend solely on testing;
* evaluate quality only at delivery time;
* separate quality from engineering;
* lose engineering evidence;
* ignore organizational learning.
