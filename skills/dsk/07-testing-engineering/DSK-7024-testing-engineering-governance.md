# DSK-7024 | Testing Engineering Governance

# 1. Purpose

This skill defines the **Testing Engineering Governance (TEG)** discipline adopted by the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Within DESys, Testing Engineering Governance is not the governance of isolated testing activities.

It is the canonical engineering discipline responsible for governing the complete Testing Engineering ecosystem, ensuring that experimentation, engineering evidence, engineering intelligence, organizational learning and continuous improvement remain permanently aligned with Engineering Excellence.

Testing Engineering Governance establishes the constitutional framework of Testing Engineering within DESys.
