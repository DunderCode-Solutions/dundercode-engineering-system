# DSK-7010 | Engineering Testing Principles

# 1. Purpose

This skill defines the **Engineering Testing Principles** adopted by the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Within DESys, testing principles are not operational recommendations or testing techniques.

They are the universal engineering principles that govern trustworthy experimentation, objective observation and evidence production throughout the engineering lifecycle.

Engineering Testing Principles establish the scientific foundation of Testing Engineering.

# 6. Engineering Testing Principles

Testing Engineering SHALL follow these principles.

## Evidence First

Engineering conclusions SHALL be supported by objective evidence.

## Hypothesis Before Experiment

Every experiment SHALL investigate an explicit engineering hypothesis.

## Controlled Experimentation

Engineering experiments SHALL minimize uncontrolled variables.

## Repeatability

Engineering experiments SHALL be repeatable by the same engineering team.

## Reproducibility

Engineering experiments SHALL produce consistent results across equivalent environments.

## Deterministic Observation

Engineering observations SHALL remain objective and measurable.

## Traceable Evidence

Every engineering observation SHALL preserve complete traceability.

## Engineering Neutrality

Engineering experiments SHALL investigate system behavior without bias.

## Continuous Learning

Engineering evidence SHALL strengthen organizational knowledge.

## Scientific Integrity

Engineering evidence SHALL never be manipulated or selectively interpreted.

These principles SHALL govern every engineering testing activity.
