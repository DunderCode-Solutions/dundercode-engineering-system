# DSK-7011 | Engineering Test Strategy

# 1. Purpose

This skill defines the **Engineering Test Strategy (ETS)** discipline adopted by the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Within DESys, a Test Strategy is not a list of testing techniques, tools or schedules.

It is the engineering discipline responsible for defining how experimentation will reduce uncertainty, produce trustworthy evidence and support engineering decisions throughout the engineering lifecycle.

Engineering Test Strategy transforms uncertainty into structured experimentation.

---

# 2. Scope

Engineering Test Strategy governs:

* Engineering Objectives
* Engineering Questions
* Risk-Based Experimentation
* Testing Hypotheses
* Evidence Planning
* Confidence Targets
* Decision Criteria
* Strategy Traceability

Engineering Test Strategy spans the complete engineering lifecycle.
