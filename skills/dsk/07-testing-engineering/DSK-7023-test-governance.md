# DSK-7023 | Test Governance

# 1. Purpose

This skill defines the **Engineering Test Governance (ETG)** discipline adopted by the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Within DESys, Test Governance is not limited to policies, approvals or compliance controls.

It is the engineering discipline responsible for directing, controlling and continuously improving the entire engineering experimentation ecosystem, ensuring alignment between engineering strategy, experimentation, evidence, intelligence and organizational objectives.

Engineering Test Governance transforms engineering experimentation into a governed organizational capability.
