# DSK-7019 | Test Analytics

# 1. Purpose

This skill defines the **Engineering Test Analytics (ETA)** discipline adopted by the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Within DESys, Test Analytics is not the activity of creating dashboards or reporting metrics.

It is the engineering discipline responsible for transforming engineering evidence into actionable engineering intelligence capable of supporting technical, architectural and organizational decisions.

Engineering Test Analytics transforms engineering evidence into engineering intelligence.

---

# 2. Scope

Engineering Test Analytics governs:

* Evidence Analytics
* Engineering Correlation
* Trend Analysis
* Risk Analytics
* Predictive Analytics
* Decision Intelligence
* Engineering Insights
* Analytics Governance

Engineering Test Analytics spans the complete engineering lifecycle.

---
