# 1. Purpose

This skill defines the **Continuous Delivery Engineering (CDE)** discipline adopted by the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Within DESys, Continuous Delivery is not a release pipeline or deployment automation.

It is the engineering discipline responsible for continuously transforming trusted engineering baselines into delivery-ready engineering capabilities through deterministic validation, release readiness assessment and controlled delivery orchestration.

Continuous Delivery Engineering transforms validated software into continuously deliverable engineering value.

---
