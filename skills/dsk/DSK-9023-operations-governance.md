# 1. Purpose

This skill defines the **Operations Governance Engineering (OGE)** discipline adopted by the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Within DESys, Operations Governance Engineering is not operational auditing, compliance reporting or administrative oversight.

It is the engineering discipline responsible for establishing, governing, validating and continuously improving the policies, controls, decision models and evidence that govern the complete Engineering Operations System.

Operations Governance Engineering transforms operational governance into a measurable engineering capability.
