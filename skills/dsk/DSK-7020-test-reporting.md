# 1. Purpose

This skill defines the **Engineering Test Reporting (ETR)** discipline adopted by the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Within DESys, Test Reporting is not the generation of static reports or dashboards.

It is the engineering discipline responsible for communicating engineering evidence, analytical insights, engineering confidence and decision recommendations to stakeholders through explainable, traceable and decision-oriented reports.

Engineering Test Reporting transforms engineering intelligence into engineering communication.
