# 1. Purpose

This skill defines the **Service Reliability Engineering (SRE)** discipline adopted by the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Within DESys, Service Reliability Engineering is not the monitoring of uptime, incident reduction or infrastructure stability.

It is the engineering discipline responsible for designing, measuring, preserving and continuously improving service reliability through measurable objectives, engineering automation, operational evidence and controlled operational risk.

Service Reliability Engineering transforms reliability into a measurable engineering capability.

---

# 17. Engineering Rules

Service Reliability Engineering MUST:

* define measurable SLIs and SLOs;
* govern engineering risk through error budgets;
* preserve reliability evidence;
* continuously improve operational confidence;
* strengthen Engineering Excellence.

Service Reliability Engineering MUST NOT:

* define unverifiable reliability objectives;
* ignore error budget consumption;
* compromise operational evidence;
* weaken reliability governance;
* tolerate unmanaged reliability degradation.
