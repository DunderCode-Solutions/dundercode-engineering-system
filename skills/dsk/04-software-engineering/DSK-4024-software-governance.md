# DSK-4024 | Software Governance

# 1. Purpose

This skill defines the **Engineering Software Governance (ESG)** model adopted by the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Within DESys, governance is not limited to administrative processes or compliance checklists.

It is the engineering discipline responsible for preserving the integrity, consistency and continuous evolution of engineering knowledge, ensuring that business objectives, domain models, architecture, design, software implementation and operational behavior remain aligned throughout the software lifecycle.

Engineering Governance protects engineering knowledge.

---

# 2. Scope

Engineering Software Governance governs:

* Engineering Policies
* Engineering Standards
* Decision Governance
* Compliance Verification
* Engineering Metrics
* Risk Management
* Engineering Knowledge Governance

---
