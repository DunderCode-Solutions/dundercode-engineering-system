# 1. Purpose

This skill establishes the canonical engineering principles governing Operations Engineering within the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Within DESys, operational excellence is not achieved through procedures alone.

It is achieved through engineering principles that guide every operational decision, operational capability and organizational behavior.

Operations Engineering Principles establish the constitutional foundation of the Engineering Operations System.

# 6. Canonical Principles

## Principle 1 — Operations as Engineering

Operations SHALL be engineered rather than administered.

Operational activities SHALL be designed as engineering capabilities.

---

## Principle 2 — Reliability First

Operational decisions SHALL prioritize long-term reliability over short-term convenience.

Reliability SHALL become a permanent engineering objective.

---

## Principle 3 — Evidence Before Assumptions

Operational decisions SHALL always be supported by engineering evidence.

Evidence SHALL prevail over intuition.

---

## Principle 4 — Observability by Default

Every operational capability SHALL expose measurable engineering signals.

Invisible operations SHALL not exist.

---

## Principle 5 — Automation Before Manual Execution

Operational work SHALL be automated whenever engineering value exists.

Manual work SHALL be minimized.

---

## Principle 6 — Recovery by Engineering

Recovery SHALL be intentionally engineered.

Operational recovery SHALL never depend upon improvisation.

---

## Principle 7 — Continuous Operational Learning

Every operational event SHALL generate organizational knowledge.

Operations SHALL continuously improve.

---

## Principle 8 — Explainable Operations

Operational decisions SHALL remain explainable.

Every engineering action SHALL preserve operational transparency.

---

## Principle 9 — Organizational Resilience

Operational capabilities SHALL strengthen organizational resilience.

Operations SHALL continue despite failures.

---

## Principle 10 — Continuous Engineering Evolution

Operations Engineering SHALL continuously evolve.

Operational maturity SHALL never become static.

---
