# 1. Purpose

This skill defines the **Engineering Quality Review (EQR)** discipline adopted by the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Within DESys, a Quality Review is not a meeting, checklist or isolated inspection activity.

It is the engineering discipline responsible for independently evaluating the effectiveness, consistency and maturity of the complete Engineering Quality System through objective evidence, structured assessment and continuous improvement.

Engineering Quality Review strengthens engineering governance.

---

# 2. Scope

Engineering Quality Review governs:

* Quality Assessment
* Engineering Reviews
* Conformance Evaluation
* Review Findings
* Engineering Recommendations
* Review Traceability
* Continuous Review
* Quality Governance Support

Engineering Quality Review applies across the complete Engineering Quality lifecycle.
