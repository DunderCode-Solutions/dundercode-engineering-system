# 1. Purpose

This skill defines the **Delivery Traceability (DT)** discipline adopted by the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Within DESys, Delivery Traceability is not the logging of deployment events or the linking of work items.

It is the engineering discipline responsible for establishing, preserving and governing the complete Engineering Delivery Chain of Custody across the Engineering Delivery System.

Delivery Traceability transforms engineering delivery into an explainable, verifiable and continuously auditable engineering capability.
