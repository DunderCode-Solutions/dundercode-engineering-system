# 1. Purpose

This skill defines the **Operational Observability Engineering (OOE)** discipline adopted by the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Within DESys, Operational Observability Engineering is not the collection of logs, dashboards or monitoring alerts.

It is the engineering discipline responsible for designing, instrumenting, correlating, interpreting and continuously evolving operational telemetry that explains the runtime behavior of engineering systems.

Operational Observability Engineering transforms telemetry into engineering intelligence.
