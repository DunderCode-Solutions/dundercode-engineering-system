# 1. Purpose

This skill defines the **Environment Engineering (EE)** discipline adopted by the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Within DESys, Environment Engineering is not the management of development, testing, staging or production environments.

It is the engineering discipline responsible for designing, governing, provisioning, reproducing, isolating, observing and continuously evolving every engineering execution environment across the complete Engineering Delivery System.

Environment Engineering transforms environments into governed engineering capabilities.

---

* Environment Architecture

Environment Engineering spans every execution context within the Engineering Delivery System.
