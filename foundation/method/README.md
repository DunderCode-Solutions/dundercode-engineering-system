# Method

The Method Domain defines how engineering work is performed within the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

It transforms engineering philosophy into structured, repeatable, and continuously improving engineering practices.

While the Canon establishes why engineering exists, the Method establishes how engineering is executed.

The Method Domain provides the engineering workflows, lifecycle, review processes, governance model, and continuous improvement practices that guide the creation and evolution of every engineering asset across the platform.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Method Domain is to establish a consistent engineering process that enables software teams to transform ideas into validated engineering assets.

Rather than relying on individual experience or ad hoc workflows, DESys defines a repeatable methodology that promotes consistency, traceability, quality, and long-term maintainability.

Every engineering activity within the platform follows the principles established by this domain.

---

# What is an Engineering Method?

An Engineering Method defines the systematic process through which engineering knowledge is created, validated, and continuously improved.

Within DESys, the method is responsible for organizing engineering activities into a coherent lifecycle that supports planning, implementation, review, publication, and continuous evolution.

Unlike engineering standards, which define what should be implemented, the engineering method defines how engineering work progresses from concept to validated knowledge.
