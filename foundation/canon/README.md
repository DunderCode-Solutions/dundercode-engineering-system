# Canon

The Canon Domain defines the philosophical foundation of the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

It contains the engineering canons that establish the beliefs, principles, values, and long-term vision guiding every engineering decision across the platform.

Within DESys, a canon represents the highest level of engineering knowledge. It provides the conceptual reference from which methodologies, standards, blueprints, and engineering practices are derived.

Rather than prescribing implementation details, the Canon Domain defines the enduring engineering principles that remain stable as technologies, tools, and software projects evolve.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Canon Domain is to establish a clear and consistent engineering philosophy for the entire DESys platform.

Every engineering asset ultimately derives its intent from the principles defined by the engineering canons.

The Canon ensures that engineering decisions remain aligned with a coherent philosophy, enabling long-term consistency across projects, teams, and technologies.

---

# What is an Engineering Canon?

An Engineering Canon is the highest-level engineering document within DESys.

A canon defines the fundamental engineering beliefs that guide every subsequent engineering asset.

Unlike standards or methodologies, canons answer foundational questions such as:

- Why does the platform exist?
- What principles should guide engineering decisions?
- What values should engineering assets preserve?
- How should engineering knowledge evolve?

Engineering canons are intentionally stable and evolve only when the engineering philosophy itself changes.
