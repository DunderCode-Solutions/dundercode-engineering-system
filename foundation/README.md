# Foundation

The Foundation Layer establishes the philosophical, methodological, and editorial principles upon which the entire DunderCode Engineering System (DESys) is built.

Every engineering asset within DESys ultimately derives its purpose, structure, and consistency from the Foundation. It defines not only how engineering knowledge is created, but also how it is communicated, validated, and continuously improved.

Rather than containing implementation guidance, the Foundation provides the conceptual framework that supports every engineering decision across the platform.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Foundation Layer is to establish a common engineering language for the DunderCode Engineering System.

It defines the principles, methodologies, and communication standards that guide the creation and evolution of every engineering asset within DESys.

By separating foundational concepts from implementation details, the Foundation ensures that engineering practices remain consistent, scalable, and technology-independent.

Every subsequent layer of the platform builds upon the principles established here.

---

# Foundation Components

The Foundation is composed of three core engineering assets.

| Engineering Asset | Responsibility |
|-------------------|----------------|
| **DEC — DunderCode Engineering Canon** | Defines the engineering philosophy, values, principles, and long-term vision of DESys. |
| **DEM — DunderCode Engineering Method** | Defines the engineering methodology, workflows, lifecycle, and development process. |
| **DCSG — DunderCode Canon Style Guide** | Defines editorial standards, terminology, writing conventions, and documentation consistency. |

Together, these documents establish the conceptual foundation of the engineering platform.

---

# DunderCode Engineering Canon (DEC)

The Engineering Canon defines the philosophical foundation of DESys.

It establishes the engineering beliefs, guiding principles, and decision-making philosophy that influence every engineering asset produced within the platform.

The Canon answers questions such as:

- Why does DESys exist?
- What principles guide engineering decisions?
- What values should engineering assets preserve?
- How should engineering knowledge evolve?

Every engineering standard ultimately traces its origin back to the Canon.

---

# DunderCode Engineering Method (DEM)

The Engineering Method transforms engineering philosophy into practical execution.

It defines how engineering work is planned, documented, reviewed, validated, and continuously improved.

The Method establishes a repeatable workflow that enables engineering teams to deliver software consistently while preserving quality and traceability.

If the Canon defines *why* engineering exists, the Method defines *how* engineering is performed.

---

# DunderCode Canon Style Guide (DCSG)

The Canon Style Guide establishes a common language for engineering communication.

It standardizes terminology, document structure, writing conventions, formatting rules, metadata, and editorial practices across the entire DESys platform.

Consistent communication enables engineering knowledge to remain understandable, maintainable, and reusable over time.

The Style Guide ensures that every engineering asset communicates knowledge with clarity and precision.

---
