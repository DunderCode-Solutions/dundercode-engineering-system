# 1. Purpose

The Continuous Improvement Standard defines the engineering requirements for using assessment results to improve engineering assets within the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Its purpose is to establish a disciplined and traceable improvement model that ensures assessment outcomes lead to meaningful engineering progress over time.

Continuous improvement is considered an engineering responsibility rather than an optional follow-up activity.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to every DAR performed under DESys.

It defines engineering expectations for feedback processing, improvement planning, follow-up action, reassessment, governance, and lifecycle evolution.

Implementation details related to project management tools, task trackers, organizational workflows, or collaboration platforms are intentionally excluded.

# 5. Continuous Improvement Principles

Continuous improvement SHALL follow the principles defined below.

## Actionability

Assessment outcomes SHOULD lead to meaningful action.

Findings without follow-up value SHOULD NOT remain unresolved.

---

## Traceability

Improvement actions SHALL remain traceable to the assessment findings that motivated them.

Improvement history SHOULD be preserved.

---

## Prioritization

Improvement actions SHOULD be prioritized according to impact, urgency, and engineering value.

Critical improvements SHOULD receive appropriate attention.

---

## Accountability

Improvement responsibilities SHALL be explicitly assigned.

Ownership MUST remain identifiable.

---

## Verification

Improvement actions SHOULD be verified after implementation.

Evidence of improvement SHOULD be collected whenever practical.

---

## Reassessment

Assets SHOULD be reassessed after material changes or improvement cycles.

Reassessment SHOULD confirm that the expected improvement occurred.

---

## Learning

Assessments SHOULD contribute to organizational learning.

Repeated patterns SHOULD inform future engineering decisions.

---

## Evolvability

Improvement processes SHALL evolve through controlled engineering practices.

Enhancements SHOULD preserve traceability and review discipline.

---

# 6. Standard

Every DAR SHALL define:

* Improvement actions
* Ownership of actions
* Prioritization approach
* Follow-up expectations
* Verification process
* Reassessment criteria
* Knowledge retention process

Projects MAY define specialized improvement workflows provided they remain consistent with the principles established by this standard.

---

# 7. Mandatory Requirements

Every assessment conducted under DESys MUST:

* Produce actionable improvements when needed.
* Assign responsibility for follow-up actions.
* Preserve traceability to findings and recommendations.
* Support reassessment after changes.
* Retain evidence of completed improvements.
* Encourage learning across future reviews.
* Integrate improvement into the assessment lifecycle.

---

# 8. Continuous Improvement Lifecycle

Continuous improvement SHALL follow a controlled lifecycle.

```text id="1c7p4v"
Assessment Outcome
      ↓
Improvement Planning
      ↓
Action Assignment
      ↓
Implementation
      ↓
Verification
      ↓
Reassessment
      ↓
Knowledge Retention
```

Improvement SHALL remain governed throughout its lifecycle.

---

# 9. Compliance

A review complies with this standard when its continuous improvement practices satisfy the requirements defined herein.

Compliance SHALL be verified during governance reviews, documentation reviews, engineering audits, and DAR quality checks.
