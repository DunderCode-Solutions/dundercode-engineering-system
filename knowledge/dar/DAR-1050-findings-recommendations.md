# DAR-1050 — Findings & Recommendations

# 1. Purpose

The Findings & Recommendations Standard defines the engineering requirements for recording assessment conclusions and improvement guidance within Documentation Assessment Reports (DAR) produced under the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Its purpose is to establish a consistent, traceable, and actionable structure for expressing assessment outcomes in a way that supports governance, decision-making, and continuous engineering improvement.

Findings and recommendations are considered engineering outcomes rather than informal commentary.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to every DAR performed under DESys.

It defines engineering expectations for finding identification, recommendation formulation, outcome recording, traceability, prioritization, and follow-up guidance.

Implementation details related to templates, workflow tools, collaboration platforms, or organizational review rituals are intentionally excluded.

# 5. Findings & Recommendations Principles

Findings and recommendations SHALL follow the principles defined below.

## Clarity

Findings and recommendations SHALL be stated clearly.

Stakeholders SHOULD understand what was observed and what is being suggested.

---

## Traceability

Each finding and recommendation SHALL remain traceable to the criteria and evidence that support it.

Assessment history SHOULD preserve this traceability.

---

## Actionability

Recommendations SHOULD provide meaningful next steps.

Guidance SHOULD be practical and relevant to the assessed asset.

---

## Objectivity

Findings SHALL be grounded in evidence rather than opinion.

Recommendations SHOULD follow logically from the observed conditions.

---

## Proportionality

Recommendations SHOULD be proportional to the significance of the finding.

Minor issues SHOULD not receive disproportionate responses.

---

## Prioritization

Findings SHOULD be prioritized according to their impact, urgency, and relevance.

Critical issues SHOULD be clearly distinguishable from improvement suggestions.

---

## Constructiveness

Recommendations SHOULD support engineering improvement.

Feedback SHOULD help strengthen future versions of the assessed asset.

---

## Consistency

Equivalent findings SHOULD produce equivalent recommendation styles whenever practical.

Assessment outcomes MUST remain comparable across reviews.

---

## Evolvability

Finding and recommendation practices SHALL evolve through controlled engineering processes.

Improvements SHOULD preserve interpretability and traceability.

---

# 6. Standard

Every DAR SHALL define:

* Finding structure
* Recommendation structure
* Priority or severity interpretation
* Traceability source
* Responsibility for follow-up
* Review completion criteria

Projects MAY define specialized finding formats provided they remain consistent with the principles established by this standard.

---

# 7. Mandatory Requirements

Every assessment conducted under DESys MUST:

* Record findings clearly.
* Provide actionable recommendations when needed.
* Preserve traceability to evidence and criteria.
* Distinguish strengths from improvement opportunities.
* Reflect the assessment outcome accurately.
* Support future reassessment.
* Align recommendations with the assessed asset and its context.

---

# 8. Findings & Recommendations Lifecycle

Findings and recommendations SHALL follow a controlled lifecycle.

```text id="d1q9nv"
Issue Identification
      ↓
Analysis
      ↓
Finding Formulation
      ↓
Recommendation Drafting
      ↓
Review
      ↓
Publication
      ↓
Follow-Up
```

Findings and recommendations SHALL remain governed throughout their lifecycle.

---

# 9. Compliance

An assessment complies with this standard when its findings and recommendations satisfy the requirements defined herein.

Compliance SHALL be verified during governance reviews, documentation reviews, engineering audits, and DAR quality checks.
