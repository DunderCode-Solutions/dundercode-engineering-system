# DAR-1080 — Assessment Governance

# 1. Purpose

The Assessment Governance Standard defines the engineering requirements for governing the full assessment lifecycle of Documentation Assessment Reports (DAR) within the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Its purpose is to establish a consistent governance model that ensures assessments remain accountable, traceable, reviewable, continuously improvable, and aligned with organizational engineering objectives.

Assessment governance is considered the organizational framework that coordinates the complete DAR Engineering Model.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to every DAR performed under DESys.

It defines engineering expectations for ownership, decision-making, review governance, traceability, compliance, auditability, and continuous improvement.

Implementation details related to organizational charts, approval tools, workflow engines, collaboration platforms, or specific management practices are intentionally excluded.

# 5. Assessment Governance Principles

Assessment governance SHALL follow the principles defined below.

## Governance by Engineering

Assessment governance SHALL be based on engineering principles rather than subjective preference.

Governance decisions MUST remain objective, consistent, and repeatable.

---

## Accountability

Assessment responsibilities SHALL be explicitly defined.

Ownership MUST remain identifiable throughout the assessment lifecycle.

---

## Controlled Decision-Making

Significant assessment decisions SHALL follow established governance processes.

Changes SHOULD be reviewed and documented.

---

## Traceability

Assessment decisions, outcomes, and governance actions SHALL remain traceable.

Assessment history SHOULD support review, auditing, and organizational learning.

---

## Compliance

Assessments SHALL comply with applicable engineering standards and governance policies.

Compliance SHOULD be periodically assessed.

---

## Standardization

Assessment processes SHOULD be standardized whenever practical.

Standardization SHOULD reduce variability while preserving necessary flexibility.

---

## Transparency

Governance processes SHOULD be understandable to relevant stakeholders.

Decision rationale SHOULD be documented and accessible.

---

## Continuous Improvement

Assessment governance SHALL evolve continuously through engineering reviews, feedback, and governance learning.

---

## Lifecycle Stewardship

Governance SHALL oversee the complete assessment lifecycle.

Governance responsibilities SHALL extend from assessment planning through knowledge retention.

---

# 6. Standard

Every DESys-compliant assessment governance model SHALL define:

* Governance responsibilities
* Decision authority
* Review process
* Compliance process
* Audit process
* Improvement process
* Traceability requirements

Projects MAY implement different governance models provided they remain consistent with the engineering principles established by this standard.

---

# 7. Mandatory Requirements

Every assessment process managed under DESys MUST:

* Define governance responsibilities.
* Preserve traceability.
* Support engineering audits.
* Maintain compliance with applicable standards.
* Produce reviewable decisions.
* Support continuous improvement.
* Preserve accountability throughout the assessment lifecycle.

---

# 8. Assessment Governance Lifecycle

Assessment governance SHALL follow a continuous engineering lifecycle.

```text
Governance Strategy
      ↓
Policy Definition
      ↓
Assessment Oversight
      ↓
Compliance Assessment
      ↓
Governance Review
      ↓
Continuous Improvement
```

Assessment governance SHALL continuously evolve alongside the DAR ecosystem.

---

# 9. Compliance

A project complies with this standard when its assessment governance practices satisfy the engineering requirements defined herein.

Compliance SHALL be verified during engineering audits, governance reviews, documentation reviews, and DAR quality checks.
