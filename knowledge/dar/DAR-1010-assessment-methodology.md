# DAR-1010 — Assessment Methodology

# 1. Purpose

The Assessment Methodology Standard defines the engineering requirements for conducting Documentation Assessment Reports (DAR) within the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Its purpose is to establish a consistent, repeatable, and evidence-based methodology for assessing engineering assets in a way that supports objective evaluation, traceability, and continuous improvement.

Assessment methodology is considered an engineering process rather than an informal review habit.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to every DAR performed under DESys.

It defines engineering expectations for assessment execution, reviewer workflow, evidence handling, evaluation steps, reporting discipline, and review completion.

Implementation details related to specific templates, software tools, automation systems, or organizational review rituals are intentionally excluded.

---

# 5. Assessment Methodology Principles

Assessment methodology SHALL follow the principles defined below.

## Structured Review

Every assessment SHALL follow a defined review structure.

Assessments MUST NOT be conducted as unstructured opinion gathering.

---

## Evidence Handling

Assessment findings SHALL be supported by relevant evidence.

Evidence SHOULD be collected before conclusions are recorded.

---

## Sequential Evaluation

Assessments SHOULD proceed through a controlled sequence of steps.

Evaluation SHOULD follow an intentional order.

---

## Reviewer Discipline

Reviewers SHALL apply the same methodology consistently.

Personal preference MUST NOT replace methodological discipline.

---

## Context Awareness

Assessments SHOULD consider the context of the evaluated engineering asset.

Findings SHOULD be interpreted according to the asset's purpose and scope.

---

## Traceable Reasoning

Assessment reasoning SHALL remain traceable to evidence, criteria, and observed conditions.

The logic behind conclusions SHOULD be understandable to stakeholders.

---

## Constructive Feedback

Assessment outcomes SHOULD support improvement.

Findings SHOULD identify strengths, gaps, and actionable next steps.

---

## Repeatability

Equivalent assets reviewed with the same methodology SHOULD produce comparable outcomes.

Methodological consistency MUST be preserved.

---

## Continuous Refinement

Assessment methodology SHALL evolve through review experience, governance learning, and process improvement.

---

# 6. Standard

Every DAR SHALL define:

* Assessment objective
* Scope of review
* Applicable criteria
* Evidence sources
* Review workflow
* Findings format
* Recommendation structure
* Completion requirements

Projects MAY adapt the assessment workflow provided they remain consistent with the methodology established by this standard.

---

# 7. Mandatory Requirements

Every DAR performed under DESys MUST:

* Follow a documented review workflow.
* Use evidence to support findings.
* Produce traceable conclusions.
* Identify strengths and improvement opportunities.
* Preserve reviewer accountability.
* Record recommendations when applicable.
* Support future reassessment.

---

# 8. Assessment Workflow

Every DAR SHALL follow a controlled workflow.

```text id="w4m7kp"
Assessment Preparation
      ↓
Asset Identification
      ↓
Evidence Collection
      ↓
Criteria Application
      ↓
Finding Analysis
      ↓
Recommendation Drafting
      ↓
Review Completion
```

The workflow SHALL remain consistent across assessments unless a justified variation is documented.

---

# 9. Compliance

An assessment complies with this standard when its methodology satisfies the requirements defined herein.

Compliance SHALL be verified during governance reviews, documentation reviews, engineering audits, and DAR quality checks.
