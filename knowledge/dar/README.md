# Documentation Assessment Report (DAR)

The Documentation Assessment Report (DAR) Domain defines the engineering review model used to evaluate documentation quality throughout the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

A DAR records the outcome of a structured review process, providing objective evidence that an engineering asset complies with the principles, standards, and quality expectations established by the platform.

Rather than serving as a simple checklist, a DAR is an engineering governance artifact that supports continuous improvement, traceability, and long-term documentation excellence.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the DAR Domain is to establish a consistent methodology for assessing engineering assets.

Every review should produce a structured assessment that documents strengths, identifies improvement opportunities, and records the overall quality of the evaluated asset.

By standardizing documentation reviews, DESys ensures that engineering knowledge evolves through measurable quality improvements.

---

# What is a DAR?

A Documentation Assessment Report (DAR) is an engineering asset that records the formal evaluation of another engineering asset.

Each DAR captures the context of the review, the evaluation criteria, the assessment outcome, and the resulting recommendations.

DARs provide a historical record of documentation quality and contribute to the governance of the engineering knowledge base.

---

# Review Philosophy

Documentation reviews within DESys follow a set of fundamental principles.

## Objectivity

Assessments should be based on observable evidence and defined evaluation criteria.

## Consistency

Equivalent engineering assets should be reviewed according to the same quality standards.

## Transparency

Review outcomes should clearly explain the reasoning behind the assessment.

## Continuous Improvement

Every review should provide actionable feedback that helps improve engineering assets.

## Traceability

Review results should remain linked to the evaluated engineering asset throughout its lifecycle.

# Review Process

The DAR process follows a structured review workflow.

```text
Engineering Asset
        │
        ▼
Engineering Review
        │
        ▼
Documentation Assessment Report
        │
        ▼
Improvement Actions
        │
        ▼
Updated Engineering Asset
```

Each review contributes to the continuous evolution of engineering quality.

---

# Review Outcomes

A DAR may produce several types of outcomes.

Typical outcomes include:

- Approved
- Approved with Recommendations
- Requires Revision
- Rejected

Each outcome reflects the overall quality of the engineering asset and guides the next steps in its lifecycle.

---

# Relationship with Engineering Assets

DARs are directly connected to the engineering assets they evaluate.

```text
Engineering Asset
        │
        ▼
Documentation Assessment Report
        │
        ▼
Review History
        │
        ▼

```

Within the DunderCode Engineering Knowledge Graph (DEKG), DARs provide governance relationships that document how engineering assets evolve over time.
