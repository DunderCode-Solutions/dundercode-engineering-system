# DAR-1000 — Assessment Principles

# 1. Purpose

The Assessment Principles Standard defines the foundational engineering principles governing every Documentation Assessment Report (DAR) produced within the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Its purpose is to establish technology-independent principles that ensure engineering assessments remain objective, consistent, evidence-based, repeatable, and continuously valuable throughout the lifecycle of engineering assets.

Assessment is considered an engineering discipline rather than a subjective review activity.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to every engineering assessment conducted under DESys, regardless of the engineering asset being evaluated.

It defines the engineering expectations that govern assessment behavior, reviewer responsibilities, evaluation integrity, evidence quality, and assessment governance.

Implementation details regarding scoring models, assessment templates, automation tools, or specific review procedures are intentionally excluded.

---

# 3. Audience

This standard is intended for:

* Engineering Reviewers
* Solution Architects

* Engineering Managers

Every stakeholder responsible for reviewing engineering assets SHALL understand and follow this standard.

# 5. Assessment Principles

Engineering assessments SHALL follow the principles defined below.

## Objectivity

Assessments SHALL be based on observable evidence rather than personal opinion.

Review conclusions MUST remain technically justifiable.

---

## Consistency

Equivalent engineering assets SHOULD receive equivalent assessments when evaluated against the same criteria.

Assessment practices SHALL remain predictable across reviewers.

---

## Evidence-Based Evaluation

Assessment findings SHALL be supported by verifiable engineering evidence.

Conclusions MUST NOT rely on assumptions alone.

---

## Traceability

Assessment decisions SHALL remain traceable to the reviewed engineering asset, evaluation criteria, and supporting evidence.

Assessment history SHOULD support future reviews and audits.

---

## Transparency

Assessment outcomes SHOULD clearly communicate both strengths and improvement opportunities.

Review reasoning SHOULD be understandable by relevant stakeholders.

---

## Fairness

Engineering assets SHALL be evaluated against established standards rather than reviewer preferences.

Equivalent situations SHOULD receive equivalent treatment.

---

## Constructiveness

Assessments SHOULD promote engineering improvement.

Findings SHOULD provide guidance that helps improve engineering quality whenever practical.

---

## Repeatability

Assessment processes SHOULD be repeatable.

Independent reviewers applying the same methodology SHOULD reach comparable conclusions.

---

## Continuous Improvement

Assessment practices SHALL evolve through engineering learning, governance reviews, and operational experience.

Assessment itself SHOULD be continuously improved.

---

# 6. Standard

Every DESys-compliant engineering assessment SHALL:

* Follow defined engineering principles.
* Be supported by observable evidence.
* Preserve reviewer independence.
* Maintain traceability.
* Promote continuous engineering improvement.
* Support governance objectives.

Projects MAY establish specialized assessment procedures provided they remain consistent with the principles established by this standard.

---

# 7. Mandatory Requirements

Every engineering assessment managed under DESys MUST:

* Have a clearly defined assessment objective.
* Use documented evaluation criteria.
* Be supported by engineering evidence.
* Preserve assessment traceability.
* Produce actionable findings.
* Maintain reviewer accountability.
* Support continuous improvement.

---

# 8. Assessment Lifecycle

Engineering assessments SHALL follow a controlled lifecycle.

```text id="z1h4nv"
Assessment Planning
        ↓
Evidence Collection
        ↓
Evaluation
        ↓
Findings
        ↓
Recommendations
        ↓
Review Completion
        ↓
Continuous Improvement
```

Assessment activities SHALL remain governed throughout their lifecycle.

---

# 9. Compliance

A review complies with this standard when its assessment process satisfies the engineering principles defined herein.

Compliance SHALL be verified during governance reviews, engineering audits, quality reviews, and Documentation Assessment Reports (DAR).
