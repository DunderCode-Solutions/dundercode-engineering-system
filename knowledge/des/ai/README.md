# AI Standards

The AI Standards define the engineering principles, practices, and governance for designing, implementing, operating, and evolving artificial intelligence capabilities within the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

AI is treated as a first-class engineering domain that includes model behavior, prompt design, knowledge integration, evaluation, safety, lifecycle management, and operational governance.

These standards establish technology-independent guidance for AI systems while remaining independent of any specific provider, model family, framework, or platform implementation.

All standards contained in this domain derive their engineering philosophy from the DunderCode Engineering Canon (DEC), the DunderCode Engineering Method (DEM), and the DunderCode Canon Style Guide (DCSG).

---

# Scope

The AI Standards cover the complete engineering model for AI-enabled systems, including:

* AI engineering principles
* Prompt engineering
* Knowledge engineering
* Model lifecycle management
* Evaluation and testing
* Safety and risk management
* Human oversight
* AI governance

These standards define engineering principles rather than prescribing specific models, vendors, APIs, or proprietary tools.

---

# Objectives

The AI Standards aim to:

* Standardize AI engineering practices.
* Improve AI system reliability and consistency.
* Reduce operational, ethical, and technical risk.
* Support safe and traceable AI adoption.
* Promote responsible AI governance.
* Enable measurable AI quality.
* Encourage continuous improvement of AI-enabled systems.

# Relationship with Other DES Domains

AI Standards integrate with multiple engineering disciplines.

* Architecture Standards define the systems that AI supports or augments.
* Data Standards define the information used by AI systems.
* Quality Standards define verification and reliability practices.
* API Standards define how AI services may be exposed and consumed.
* Observability Standards define how AI systems are understood in operation.
* Cloud Standards define the infrastructure on which AI systems run.

AI engineering transforms knowledge, architecture, and operational data into governed intelligent capabilities.

---

# Compliance

Projects developed under DESys SHOULD comply with the AI Standards applicable to their architecture, operational requirements, and AI deployment model.

Compliance is evaluated through engineering reviews, AI assessments, safety reviews, governance reviews, and DunderCode Assessment Reports (DAR).
