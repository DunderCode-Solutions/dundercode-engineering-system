# 1. Purpose

The AI Governance Standard defines the engineering requirements for governing artificial intelligence systems throughout their lifecycle within the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Its purpose is to establish technology-independent engineering principles that ensure AI systems remain accountable, traceable, safe, compliant, reliable, and continuously improvable while aligning with organizational objectives.

AI governance is considered the organizational framework that coordinates every AI engineering discipline defined within DESys.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to every AI-enabled system, AI model, prompt, knowledge asset, operational workflow, and governance activity managed under DESys.

It defines engineering expectations for governance structures, ownership, oversight, compliance, decision-making, lifecycle management, auditing, and continuous improvement.

Implementation details related to specific AI platforms, governance software, regulatory frameworks, provider-specific policies, or organizational management systems are intentionally excluded.

# 5. AI Governance Principles

AI governance SHALL follow the principles defined below.

## Governance by Engineering

AI governance SHALL be based on engineering principles rather than subjective decisions.

Governance decisions MUST remain consistent, objective, and repeatable.

---

## Accountability

Responsibilities for AI systems SHALL be explicitly defined.

Ownership MUST remain identifiable throughout the AI lifecycle.

---

## Controlled Decision-Making

Material AI decisions SHALL follow established engineering processes.

Significant changes SHOULD be reviewed before adoption.

---

## Compliance

AI systems SHALL comply with applicable engineering standards and organizational governance policies.

Compliance SHOULD be reviewed periodically.

---

## Traceability

Governance decisions, approvals, and lifecycle events SHALL remain traceable.

Governance history SHOULD support auditing, engineering review, and organizational learning.

---

## Transparency

Governance processes SHOULD be understandable by relevant stakeholders.

Responsibilities and decision authority SHOULD be clearly documented.

---

## Risk Awareness

AI governance SHALL consider operational, technical, ethical, and organizational risks.

Governance SHOULD ensure that risks are identified, reviewed, and appropriately managed.

---

## Continuous Improvement

AI governance SHALL evolve through engineering reviews, operational feedback, safety assessments, and organizational learning.

Governance SHOULD continuously improve as AI capabilities evolve.

---

## Lifecycle Stewardship

Governance SHALL oversee the complete AI lifecycle.

Governance responsibilities SHALL extend from AI conception through retirement.

---

# 6. Standard

Every DESys-compliant AI governance model SHALL define:

* Governance responsibilities
* Decision authority
* Ownership model
* Review process
* Compliance process
* Safety oversight
* Operational oversight
* Continuous improvement process
* Audit process
* Traceability requirements

Projects MAY implement different AI governance models provided they remain consistent with the engineering principles established by this standard.

---

# 7. Mandatory Requirements

Every AI governance process managed under DESys MUST:

* Define governance responsibilities.
* Preserve accountability.
* Support engineering audits.
* Maintain compliance with applicable standards.
* Preserve traceability.
* Support continuous governance reviews.
* Promote continuous improvement.

---

# 8. AI Governance Lifecycle

AI governance SHALL follow a continuous engineering lifecycle.

```text id="v8mq2p"
Governance Strategy
        ↓
Policy Definition
        ↓
Engineering Oversight
        ↓
Compliance Assessment
        ↓
Operational Review
        ↓
Continuous Improvement
```

AI governance SHALL continuously evolve alongside AI systems.

---

# 9. Compliance

A project complies with this standard when its AI governance practices satisfy the engineering requirements defined herein.

Compliance SHALL be verified during architecture reviews, AI assessments, governance reviews, engineering audits, operational reviews, and DunderCode Assessment Reports (DAR).
