# DunderCode Engineering Processes (DEP)

This collection is a navigation surface for draft process and practice
candidates. Practices describe possible ways to organize work; they are not
engineering standards and do not establish a universal process, approval
sequence, or role model.

## Authority and Lifecycle

When vendored into a consumer repository, this material is reference-only.
Consumer code, runtime evidence, approved decisions, policies, and legal
obligations remain authoritative for that project. Consumers decide through
their own governance which practices to adopt, tailor, replace, or reject and
who may approve engineering or production changes.

The DEP documents remain drafts. This README does not approve them or advance
their lifecycle state, and unreviewed process documents are not presented here
as public guidance. Standards candidates express proposed technical
expectations; practices may offer implementation approaches only after separate
review.
