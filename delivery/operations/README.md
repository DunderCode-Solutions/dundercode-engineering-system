# Operations

The Operations Domain defines the engineering practices required to operate, maintain, and sustain software systems throughout their production lifecycle within the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Operations ensure that deployed software remains available, reliable, secure, and performant while supporting continuous business operations.

Rather than deploying software, this domain focuses on keeping software running effectively in production.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Operations Domain is to establish standardized operational practices that ensure software systems remain healthy, resilient, and continuously available.

By defining operational procedures, maintenance strategies, and service management practices, DESys enables engineering teams to maintain production environments with consistency, predictability, and operational excellence.

Operations extend engineering quality beyond deployment into daily production management.

---

# Operations Principles

Operations within DESys follow a common set of engineering principles.

## Reliability

Production services should operate consistently and predictably.

## Availability

Critical systems should maximize service availability.

## Resilience

Operational processes should minimize the impact of failures.

## Recoverability

Operational procedures should support rapid recovery from incidents.

## Automation

Operational activities should be automated whenever practical.

## Continuous Improvement

Operational experience should continuously improve engineering practices and platform reliability.

---

# Operational Responsibilities

Operations encompass the continuous management of production environments.

Typical operational responsibilities include:

- Service administration.
- Environment management.
- Backup and recovery.
- Capacity planning.
- Infrastructure maintenance.
- Configuration management.
- High availability.
- Disaster recovery.
- Performance optimization.
- Operational documentation.

Together, these responsibilities ensure that production environments remain stable and reliable.

# Relationship with the Delivery Layer

```text

        │
        ▼
Release
        │
        ▼
Deployment
        │
        ▼
Operations
        │
        ▼

        │
        ▼

```

Deployment installs software.

Observability measures software behavior.

Support assists users and resolves incidents.
