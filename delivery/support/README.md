# Support

The Support Domain defines the engineering practices used to assist users, resolve operational issues, and continuously improve software systems throughout their lifecycle within the DunderCode Engineering System (DESys).

Support extends engineering beyond software delivery by ensuring that production systems remain useful, reliable, and continuously improved through structured incident response, user assistance, and operational feedback.

Rather than operating software, this domain focuses on supporting both the software and the people who depend on it.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Support Domain is to establish standardized processes for handling incidents, service requests, operational issues, and user feedback.

By providing structured support practices, DESys enables engineering teams to restore services quickly, improve customer experience, preserve operational knowledge, and continuously evolve software based on real-world usage.

Support transforms operational experience into engineering improvement.

---

# Support Principles

Support within DESys follows a common set of engineering principles.

## User-Centered

Support activities should prioritize restoring value to users as quickly as possible.

## Timeliness

Incidents and service requests should be addressed within defined service objectives.

## Knowledge Sharing

Solutions should be documented to improve future response and organizational learning.

## Root Cause Analysis

Recurring issues should be investigated beyond their immediate symptoms.

## Continuous Improvement

Support outcomes should generate improvements to engineering practices, software quality, and operational procedures.

## Traceability

Support activities should remain traceable from initial report through final resolution.
